TrackA'gain

A workout tracker that tells you what to lift next.

TrackA'gain plans your sets, watches your reps, and suggests when to add weight — so you spend your energy on the workout, not the spreadsheet.

Download APK Free · Android 8.0+ · No account needed

Nothing leaves your phone. Every set, split and PR is stored locally — no sign-up, no cloud, no ads.

What it does

Progressive overload, calculated for you

After a few clean sessions at your target reps, it tells you exactly when — and by how much — to add weight, tuned to your training goal.

Rest timer that follows the plan

A countdown with alerts between sets, using your own default or a per-exercise override you set once.

Every PR, tracked automatically

No manual logging of records — every set is checked against your history the moment you log it.

Supersets and drop sets

Group exercises together on the fly, or log a multi-stage drop set exactly as it happened.

See where the work is going

A muscle-by-muscle breakdown of effective sets, so you know when a group is being neglected before it shows.

Splits that flex with you

Build a Push/Pull/Legs or Upper/Lower week, save it as a preset, and swap between them whenever your routine changes.

Your training data is yours. Full stop.

TrackA'gain doesn't ask for an account because it doesn't need one. There's no server behind it — everything you log lives in the app on your device.

Installing on Android

Since this isn't distributed through the Play Store, Android will ask you to confirm the install manually — that's expected.

  1. 1

    Tap Download APK above

    Your browser may warn that this file type can harm your device — that warning appears for any app installed outside the Play Store. Choose Download anyway.

  2. 2

    Open the downloaded file

    Find it in your Downloads folder or notification shade and tap it to begin installing.

  3. 3

    Allow this source, if asked

    Android may ask to let your browser or Files app install unknown apps. Tap Settings, enable it for that app, then go back and continue.

  4. 4

    Tap Install, then open

    That's it — no account, no setup screen. Build your first split and get moving.
